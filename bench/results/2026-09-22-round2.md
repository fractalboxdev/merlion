## Tried and reverted

- **Straightening one-to-one runs after Brandes–Köpf.** Each run moves as one piece to the free position with the most straight segments. It gave bends 4.89 → 4.85, stress 0.0719 → 0.0718 and area −0.3%, but stability p95 went from 0.254 to 0.269 (`demos-flowchart-62` remove-edge 0.066 → 0.091). It also left the count of visibly bent one-to-one edges at 55 of 469. Most of those bends come from port spreading, not from coordinates: a node with two edges on its bottom side gives even the aligned edge an off-centre port (`e2e-dagre-…-79` `san_francisco → decide`, `demos-flowchart-13` `C → D`).
- **Exact refinement with `k = 16`** instead of 8 (`specs/layout.md` §3 pass 2) changes no corpus metric.

## Gaps left, and why

- **Port spreading bends aligned edges.** `specs/layout.md` §6 spreads ports "evenly along the node side". 55 of 469 one-to-one edges still bend, and in the cases above the end nodes are aligned and the bend is the port offset. Bends stay at 4.9 against 4.2 for ELK, with 73 losses. Giving a port directly above or below its other end its straight position needs a spec change to §6.
- **Row reflow when packing components.** `demos-flowchart-37` rename-label moves 0.174 against 0 for ELK. It accounts for most of the rename mean of 0.033, against 0.004 for ELK. Widening `A1` pushes `F1` into the second row and `K1` into the third, so every component after `A1` moves. The hint carries layer order only (`specs/svg-output.md#layout-hint`), so row breaks are not reused.
- **SCC entry is the first declared node.** In `e2e-dagre-…-79` the loop is entered at `san_francisco`, the first declared node, while mermaid enters it at `stockholm`, the source of the first edge. This follows `specs/layout.md` §1, and the long back-edge from `new_york` runs around the whole drawing (stress 0.24).
- **Dominator relayering on add-edge.** `demos-flowchart-62--add-edge` moves 0.317 against 0.018 for ELK (`specs/layout.md` §1–2, unchanged since round 1).
- **Config keys.** `flowchart.nodeSpacing`/`rankSpacing` (5 diagrams) and `wrappingWidth` (2) are outside the accepted front-matter keys (`specs/parser.md`). Honouring them would move area in both directions (`nodeSpacing: 1` and `250`) and would not make `e2e-selected-shape-wrapping` fit, because step 4 already tries wrap widths down to 120 px.
- **Compat 34 misses**: unchanged from round 1.
