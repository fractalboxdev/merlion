# Benchmark: improvement round 1

Compat corpus (390 flowcharts, mermaid `98a0945418c7`, tag `mermaid@12.0.0`) and 106 edit pairs, all three renderers re-run on 2026-09-22. Baseline is commit `b67f059`; the result is commit `181567f`. Metric definitions: `bench/README.md`; method: `specs/benchmark.md`. mermaid-dagre and mermaid-elk numbers are unchanged between the runs apart from timing.

**Stress.** The mean and median move only because 12 diagrams now have a different, correct graph: the parser change removes phantom nodes that named a subgraph (isolated vertices at stress 0) and connects their edges to the subgraph. On the 293 diagrams whose node and edge counts are unchanged, mean stress is 0.0722 before and 0.0724 after, and the median is 0.0305 in both. The median of the whole set jumps from 0.007 to 0.030 because 125–128 diagrams sit at exactly 0 and the median falls on the edge of that cluster.

**Speed.** The single-run p95 is 0.3 ms before and 1.0 ms after, but the host ran at load average 4–7 during these runs. Alternating the two binaries, with each diagram timed as the minimum of 7 batch runs, gives p95 475–684 µs for `b67f059` and 404–762 µs for `181567f` over three pairs. The ranges overlap, so no speed change is measurable at this noise level.

## Changes, one commit each

Mean values after each commit, cumulative from the baseline. Stress is omitted because it only moves with the graph changes described above.

| Commit | Change | Crossings | Label overlaps | Compat | Area | Stability mean / p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `b67f059` | baseline | 1.828 | 0.102 | 340 | 114463 | 0.092 / 0.482 |
| `10eb2cc` | Orthogonal jogs in a gap are ordered by the legs they would cross (`specs/layout.md` §6) | 1.224 | 0.107 | 340 | 114463 | 0.092 / 0.482 |
| `d184d9f` | Wrap detours nest: at each boundary the step leaving furthest along the order axis takes the inner channel and gap tracks and the outer entry track (§5 step 2) | 0.219 | 0.096 | 340 | 113835 | 0.093 / 0.482 |
| `62da66e` | Label space uses the drawn chip size, and side-by-side labels between neighbouring layers are stacked across a widened gap (§6) | 0.219 | 0.005 | 340 | 115252 | 0.098 / 0.411 |
| `67c13e2` | Subgraph ids named as nodes resolve to the subgraph, nest it when named inside another subgraph, and edges between a subgraph and its member are dropped (`specs/parser.md#compatibility`) | 0.219 | 0.005 | 346 | 115125 | 0.098 / 0.411 |
| `181567f` | mermaid's label-less symbol shapes (`sm-circ`, `f-circ`, `fr-circ`, `cross-circ`, `fork`, `hourglass`, `bolt`) are drawn at a fixed size without a label | 0.219 | 0.005 | 355 | 114988 | 0.098 / 0.411 |

- **Jog order.** In one gap, jog a runs above jog b when a's upper leg lies inside b's span, and below b when a's lower leg does. A greedy order over those preferences leaves only the crossings that a cycle of them forces. This fixes `e2e-dagre-7213` (54 crossings to 0), `e2e-dagre-6088-2` (6 to 0) and `e2e-6617` (2 to 0). One chip in `e2e-dagre-6080` moves onto another (overlaps 0 to 2); the next commit fixes it.
- **Wrap nesting.** Parallel detours around a wrap no longer cross. `demos-flowchart-07`/`08`/`e2e-6` go from 116 crossings to 0, `demos-flowchart-01`/`02`/`45`/`e2e-12` from 10 to 0, and `expanded-node-shapes-01`/`02` from 20 to 0.
- **Label chips.** The layout reserved text + 4 px, but the SVG chip is text + (8, 4) px, so chips reached up to 2 px into node boxes. In `e2e-with-formatting-in-a-node`, for example, chip `1o` sits 1.4 px inside node `a`. Two labelled edges between the same pair of nodes, such as `e2e-74`, now stack instead of sharing one spot. Against the baseline, 21 of the 23 diagrams with overlaps reach 0, and `demos-flowchart-39`/`40` go from 2 to 1. The cost is area +1.2% and edge length +1.3%, because gaps grow to fit the chips. Stability moves mean +0.005 and p95 −0.071. The largest movements are in `demos-flowchart-37`/`39`/`40`, whose split points shift with the gap sizes.
- **Subgraph ids.** Six compat misses are fixed: `e2e-50`/`52` (+ dagre twins), `e2e-5064` and `e2e-elk-container-algorithms-…`.
- **Symbol shapes.** Nine compat misses are fixed: `docs-flowchart-41`/`42`/`43`/`44`/`48`/`57`/`66` and `expanded-node-shapes-01`/`02`.

## Gaps left, and why

- **Wraps that do not reach 720 px.** Stress on `demos-flowchart-07`/`08`/`e2e-6` is 0.352 against 0.281 for ELK, at 949 px wide. `specs/layout.md` §5 step 2 repeats the wrap "while the result is still too wide", so a partial wrap is kept even when nothing fits. The TB counterpart is tested as intended (`tb_split_narrows_a_wide_fan_and_keeps_edges_downward`). Keeping the unwrapped drawing when no step fits would need a spec change.
- **Stability.** `specs/svg-output.md#layout-hint` numbers hint layers before container fit, "so a change in fit doesn't change the hint". Split and wrap points are therefore not reused, and `demos-flowchart-37` still moves 0.24–0.30 per edit. `demos-flowchart-62--add-edge` (0.313 against ELK's 0.018) comes from dominator layering (§1–2). The added `A --> H` bypasses the loop header `B`, so `H → B` stops being a back-edge and every layer below `B` shifts.
- **Compat, 34 misses left.** Five are the `E002` parse failures (trailing `.`, leading `-`). The other 29 are:
  - collapsed subgraph view (`view: collapsed`, 6)
  - an empty subgraph that mermaid draws as a node (`e2e-57-…-4` + twin, 2)
  - HTML tags kept literal under `htmlLabels: true` (3)
  - markdown applied to a plain string (1)
  - the implicit edge id `L_B_D_0@{}` (1)
  - the 16 reference and extractor artefacts listed in the baseline
- **Component packing.** Only 9 corpus diagrams have more than one component and a natural width over 720 px. In the largest case, `expanded-node-shapes-01`/`02`, six LR chains of 8 share their layers, so each chain alone is as wide as the whole drawing (1132 px) and still wraps.
